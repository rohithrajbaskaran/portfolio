How I work

Three things I hold myself to.

01

Measure the outcome, not the output.

"Shipped a search feature" is output. "Incident search 20% faster" is outcome. I scope work around the number that matters.

02

Trust, but verify, then automate.

From 92% test coverage to audit trails on AI findings, I'd rather prove something works than assume it does.

03

Keep humans in the loop.

AI should speed up judgment, not replace it. My agents flag and explain; people approve and own the decision.
